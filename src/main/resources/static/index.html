How Resume Analyzer AI Works

We use Gemini (Google's latest LLM) to simulate a real recruiter or technical screener.

  • 1. OCR Text Extraction: If you upload an image (JPG/PNG) or a scanned PDF, we first run Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to read the text.
  • 2. AI Analysis: Your resume text is combined with your Target Job & Description, and sent to the Gemini API to analyze it against strict ATS constraints.
  • 3. Privacy: We perform the analysis in-memory. We never save or store your resume on our servers after the analysis is complete.
  • 4. Limitations: The system works best with English (or Hinglish) text. Heavily graphic, multi-column resumes or very blurry photos may lose structure or text during extraction.
